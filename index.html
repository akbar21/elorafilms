About Elora Films

Stories from
the region.

Elora Films is an independent film platform focused on developing, producing and connecting distinctive stories from Southeast Asia.

We believe in bold voices.

Elora Films works with filmmakers who bring personal perspectives, strong visual language and stories rooted in the realities of contemporary Asia.

From development to production and festival distribution, we build creative collaborations that allow each project to retain its unique voice.

Our work moves between Indonesia and the wider Southeast Asian film community, creating space for emerging and established filmmakers to meet.
